# Classify AI

Transaction classification that combines transaction history, merchant enrichment, and custom charts of accounts.

Retrieval-augmented bookkeeping · Production

Reduce manual transaction categorization and connect classification to the wider bookkeeping workflow.

Joined as the first AI Product Engineer and led a three-engineer team from prototype to production on Classify AI; processed 50K+ transactions daily and reduced manual categorization by approximately 80%.

Built retrieval-augmented classification with LangChain, Pinecone, Elasticsearch, Redis, Celery, and Django, combining transaction history, merchant enrichment, and custom charts of accounts.

Evolved bookkeeping from CSV upload to Plaid/Teller bank ingestion, OCR-supported statements, reconciliation, and QuickBooks push; helped reduce first-month close from 4+ months to approximately 2 weeks.
