# Prefer focused agents with bounded responsibilities

A small agent is easier to inspect than a universal assistant.

Reading takeaway: keep an agent focused on a specific job and its required context. The notes connect narrower scope with manageable histories, clearer responsibilities, and easier debugging. Test that claim on the actual task rather than treating size as a guarantee.
