# Bank Connections

Reusable banking infrastructure · Finally engineering

Move bookkeeping beyond CSV uploads with reusable account connections and normalized bank data.

Architected reusable Plaid/Teller infrastructure for account linking, encrypted token storage, token lifecycle management, webhooks, transaction synchronization, and normalized account data.

Evolved bookkeeping from CSV upload to Plaid/Teller bank ingestion, OCR-supported statements, reconciliation, and QuickBooks push; helped reduce first-month close from 4+ months to approximately 2 weeks.
